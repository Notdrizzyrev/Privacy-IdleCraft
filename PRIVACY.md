## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 13, 2026

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how IdleCraft processes information when you use the Discord bot and its features.

### 2. Information We May Collect or Process

Depending on the features you use, IdleCraft may process:
- **Discord identifiers** - user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs (required to operate commands and persistent control panels).
- **Configuration information** - server preferences, registered profiles, access settings, and feature configurations.
- **Minecraft connection information** - server addresses, ports, versions, connection status, and session history (where applicable).
- **Operational information** - error logs, timestamps, reconnect attempts, uptime statistics, and security/moderation logs (where applicable).
- **Support information** - any information you voluntarily provide when requesting assistance.

### 3. How Information Is Used

We use this information to:
- Provide and operate IdleCraft's features
- Manage Minecraft bot sessions
- Maintain Discord control panels
- Verify permissions
- Record uptime and troubleshooting data
- Protect the service from abuse
- Respond to support requests

### 4. Data Storage

IdleCraft may store data using MongoDB or other configured storage systems. The specific data stored depends on the current configuration.

### 5. Third-Party Services

IdleCraft relies on Discord for bot functionality and may use database, hosting, or other infrastructure providers. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

### 6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with:
- Necessary service providers to operate IdleCraft
- Law enforcement or authorities when legally required
- Other users only when access controls are properly enforced (users in one Discord server cannot access restricted data from another server)

### 7. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain information as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

If you would like to request access to or deletion of your information, contact the IdleCraft team through the official support Discord server:

**https://discord.gg/MfwFebzdpc**

Requests may require verification. Some records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.

### 8. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect stored information. However, no electronic system or online service can be guaranteed 100% secure.

### 9. Children's Privacy

IdleCraft is not intended for users under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child in violation of applicable laws, please contact us through the support Discord server.

### 10. International Processing

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than your own, subject to applicable legal requirements.

### 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our features or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at the official repository URL and will take effect upon the updated "Last Updated" date.

### 12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the IdleCraft team via the official support Discord server:

**https://discord.gg/MfwFebzdpc**
